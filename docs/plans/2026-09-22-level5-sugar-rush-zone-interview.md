# Level 5 — The Sugar Rush Zone (Wes interview, 2026-09-22)

Wes (5) designed Level 5 in an interview on 2026-09-22. This file records his answers. The design doc and the plan come next.

## Wes's answers

| Topic | Wes picked | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place | Candy Land | A world made of candy: lollipops, cupcakes, donuts, a chocolate river. |
| Name | The Sugar Rush Zone | The level title. |
| Zombies | Gummy bear, gumball, chocolate | See the bestiary below. |
| Special thing | Bouncy marshmallows | Giant marshmallows act as trampolines and launch the player high. |
| Boss | Gummy Worm King | A giant gummy worm that digs under the ground and pops up to chomp. |
| How to win | Feed him a sour candy | A sour candy in his mouth makes him dizzy. Then the player hits him. |

## Bestiary

- **Gummy bear zombie:** squishy and bouncy. A hit splits it into two small gummy bears.
- **Gumball zombie:** round. It rolls at the player very fast, like a bowling ball.
- **Chocolate zombie:** it melts into a puddle and pops up behind the player.

## Boss fight idea

1. The ground shakes to warn the player.
2. The Gummy Worm King pops up and tries to chomp.
3. The player gets a sour candy into his open mouth.
4. He makes a sour face and gets dizzy. This is the damage window.
5. The player slashes his head. He digs back under, and the loop repeats.

## Open questions for the design session

- Where does the player get the sour candy? Options: it drops from gumball zombies, it grows in the arena, or the player hits a sour candy that the King spits out.
- The first design doc planned Level 5 as "The Dark Underworld" with Henry's Zombie Taco Truck boss. Does the taco truck move to a later level?
- The first design doc put the 5-key portal at the end of Level 5. Does the portal stay in the Sugar Rush Zone?
- Which power monsters spawn here? The level tests need all four buff types in the game.
